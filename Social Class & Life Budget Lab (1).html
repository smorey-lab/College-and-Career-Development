Handout: Simulation Analysis

Use your results to respond to the original Social Class and Stratification Simulation prompts. Responses are not graded; they help you explain your reasoning.

1. Social class

Based on your annual salary and job description, what part of the U.S. social class structure might you fall into (upper, middle, or lower)? Explain what else besides salary could affect this classification.

2. Extracurricular activities

What two activities could you afford in your free time? Include estimated costs and compare them with your weekly remaining budget.

3. Budget cuts

What might need to be reduced or cut to cover basic necessities if your budget is negative?

4. Having children

The handout estimates childcare at $18,000 per year. How does adding $1,500 per month change your remaining income?

5. A spouse with similar income

How does a second similar income change affordability, savings, recreation, and quality of life? What new costs might a two-person household have?

6. AXES writing response

Explain what life might be like at this social class level in America. Would you be able to save, vacation, start a family, or buy a home? How easy would it be to move up the class structure? Support your response with evidence from your budget.
